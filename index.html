About me

I'm a software developer with a passion for building reliable, efficient, and user-focused applications. I enjoy solving complex problems, debugging challenging issues, and continuously learning new technologies. My primary focus is backend development with Java and Spring Boot, but I also have experience with multiple programming languages and frameworks, including C#, C++, C, Python, and PHP, as well as ASP.NET. On the frontend side, I work with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, Angular, Vue.js, and GWT to create responsive and user-friendly interfaces. I enjoy working across the entire development lifecycle—from designing and implementing new features to optimizing existing systems and improving application performance. I believe that clean, maintainable code and attention to detail are the foundation of great software. Whether I'm fixing bugs, developing new functionality, improving system stability, or collaborating with a team, I strive to create solutions that are both robust and easy to maintain. Outside of coding, I enjoy exploring new technologies, improving my skills, and taking on projects that challenge me to grow as a developer.
